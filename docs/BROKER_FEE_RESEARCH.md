# Offline IBIE fee research

The `fees` command calculates unrounded research costs from the **11 November 2026** IBIE Pro US-stock schedule. It does not load account configuration, open a database, connect a provider, convert cash or submit an order. The existing paper engine's fee model is unchanged. This calculator is not a historical fee database or a broker invoice.

```powershell
python -B -m src.runtime.paper_cli fees --pricing tiered --quantity 10 --price 100 --stamp-rate 0 --finra-pass-through listed --prefund-usd 10000 --eur-per-usd 0.90
```

Prices, quantities and FX rate in this command are arithmetic assumptions, not market observations. Output separates components, flat-price round-trip friction and the minimum non-negative total gross gain needed to cover costs at the actual higher exit price. `--extra-usd` adds a stated required profit or overhead allocation. Changing to `--pricing fixed` selects its separate commission/third-party treatment. Entry/exit liquidity defaults to `add`/`remove`; the Tiered venue proxy is a displayed Nasdaq-listed execution on Nasdaq, not guaranteed SmartRouting.

Both the Portuguese commission-tax scenario and the ambiguous Q4 FINRA commission multiplier must be chosen explicitly. `--stamp-rate 0` is not an exemption finding; `0.04` adds a sensitivity of 4% of brokerage commission and, in the optional conversion example, its separately charged FX commission. Collection and the actual taxable base for a Portuguese private individual were not established. FINRA per-share TAF is zero in this Q4 snapshot, while `--finra-pass-through zero|listed` selects the separate commission multiplier interpretation.

The public Tiered maximum is inconsistent between table and disclosure. Cap-sensitive tickets are refused unless `--tiered-cap 0.005` or `0.01` selects the interpretation explicitly. A minimum is applied before the selected maximum, so the maximum can override it. Fixed uses the unambiguous 1% cap. Whole-share profiles exclude fractional-share, alternate/direct-route and special order programmes. Tiered break-even solving requires entry price at least USD 1, keeping it in a continuous venue-fee band. Below-dollar homogeneous order cost calculations remain available through the Python API; mixed bands cannot be priced from average price alone.

`order_fees` is a stateless cumulative single-order liability for a specified average price, route, liquidity and price-fee band. For partial fills, an incremental commission is the new cumulative commission less the prior one, not a new minimum on every execution. Mixed venues, fee bands or liquidity need separate external-fill attribution while retaining the shared order minimum. Modified and overnight orders can restart the minimum under broker rules. No partial-fill state or fee persistence is integrated into the account by this module. `monthly_shares_before` counts qualifying volume already executed this month; the round-trip helper advances it after the buy leg. Large-volume discounts are calculations, not an assumption about a small account's actual activity.

Manual currency conversion charges its commission in the account base currency, EUR in this example. The USD 2 minimum is a USD-equivalent amount, so at the assumed EUR 0.90 per USD the native fee is EUR 1.80. It is one charge per conversion batch, not per stock round trip. USD holdings can avoid repeated conversion, but new conversion proceeds require their actual settlement value date. Automatic conversion normally applies a 0.03% rate adjustment with no separate manual commission. Spreads, account eligibility and actual settlement availability remain separate inputs.

The pure `feasibility_envelope` helpers provide cash/timing upper bounds and an equicorrelated Gaussian benchmark. They never establish achievable fills, an independent trade count or an investment return. Unsettled-proceeds reuse is a counterfactual, not deployable cash-account permission. A clock error, closed session, whole-share rounding, costs, liquidity or capacity can make actual trade counts much smaller. Fixed overhead and no-trade days remain in all-in results.

New recordings carry **quote_policy_version 2**, separately from financial execution semantics 2. The long-only proposal correction changes rejection traces even where cash/fills happen to match. A recording missing the quote-policy field is legacy version 1 and requires the existing explicit `--research-assumption-override` to re-evaluate it. Results identify the original/current quote policy and `corrected_re_evaluation`; no account-version bump or private migration is performed. Old recordings are not relabelled or overwritten.
